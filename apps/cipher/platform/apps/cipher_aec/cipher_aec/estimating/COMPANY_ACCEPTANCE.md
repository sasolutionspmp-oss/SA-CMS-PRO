Company estimates and takeoff use the existing canonical records and calculator. Explicit `estimating.company_api` and `takeoff.company_api` methods select Company scope; their requests contain no Project or scope override. Stored Company records declare `scope_type: "Company"` and `project: null`. A missing Project on a Project route never selects Company. Project input schemas, record bodies and calculation hashes retain their existing form.

Company unit manifests use `cipher.canonical.v2`, and Company calculation inputs use `cipher.estimate.inputs.v2`. Qualified native UOMs, immutable rate/assembly/conversion revisions and exact rational arithmetic are shared. Current native UOM/Currency read rights apply to both scopes, including retained records and lists. Historical facts may remain readable when a native master is disabled; new calculations and acceptance require enabled units and currency. Company estimates may retain an immutable, currently authorized same-company `pursuit_id`.

The shared `estimate.accept.v1` action targets an exact `Cipher Estimate Revision` UUID and its digest. Independent human review grants authority; the requester consumes that exact grant atomically. `Cipher Estimate Acceptance` stores an immutable result, manifest, revision/snapshot references, independent person, packet/decision and predecessor. It does not rewrite the draft or original snapshot. `get_record(kind="acceptance")` and `list_acceptances` expose authorized original baselines.

Acceptance names two policies. `pinned-revision-at-pricing-date` keeps the selected rate, assembly, conversion and citation revisions exact; unrelated quote successors do not retarget them. `current-working-head-v1` requires attached drawing/calibration/measurement dependencies to remain eligible for new use. The manifest also binds the current estimate pointer, complete subsequently attached binding inventory, native source versions, qualified units, currency policy and Pursuit provenance. Every calibration/depth/output unit in bound geometry is included. A changed declared dependency invalidates pending authority. Accepted historical values never automatically reprice.

Historical packet/baseline/receipt readers authorize the stored original manifest and current rights to its native and document sources. They permit retained old drawing heads and disabled historical units. Company approval and estimate duties remain separate from Project duties. A later award must explicitly adopt the Company baseline while preserving its origin; no source record is retargeted to a Project.

The implementation uses deterministic authored inputs and bounded PDF geometry. It makes no live model, OCR, CAD, licensed pricing-feed, provider, contractual issuance, budget adoption or financial-posting claim. Native synthetic Session fixtures, actual cookie/CSRF HTTP evidence and pure numerical fixtures are recorded separately.

Generated commercial proposal PDFs currently remain readable through their producer source authority, but are explicitly refused as new EST/takeoff inputs with `source_dependency_unqualified`. Their full producer unit/currency/native dependency union must be integrated and qualified before that admission is enabled. Ordinary raw canonical Company artifacts remain supported. This boundary prevents eager generated-source authorization from acquiring shared master locks before the outer estimate's complete ordered union.
